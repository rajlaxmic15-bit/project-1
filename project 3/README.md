## Setup Instructions

### IMPORTANT: Save Your Hero Image First! 🎯

**Option 1 - Quick Setup (Recommended):**
1. Open `setup-image.html` in your browser
2. Follow the instructions to save your handshake image as `hero-image.jpg`
3. Open `index.html` to see your complete website!

**Option 2 - Manual Setup:**
1. Find the business handshake image you uploaded
2. Copy it to this project folder
3. Rename it to `hero-image.jpg` (exactly this name)
4. Open `index.html` in your browser

### 2. Add Your Logo (Optional)
Place your logo as `logo.png` in the root directory. The site will display "Wealth Acumen" text if no logo is found.

### 3. You're Done! 🎉
Open `index.html` in your web browser and enjoy your new website!

## Customization

### Update Contact Information
Edit the contact section in `index.html` to add your real:
- Address
- Phone number
- Email
- Social media links

### Modify Services
Edit the services section in `index.html` to match your specific offerings.

### Change Colors
Update the CSS variables in `styles.css`:
- Primary Blue: `#1a5490`
- Accent Orange: `#ff6b35`
